Estimator sampling distributions

Follow repeated samples from a uniform population to compare two ways of estimating its unknown upper endpoint.

The problem

A source generates independent observations from a uniform distribution. The right endpoint, θ, is unknown. How should we estimate it?

X1, …, Xn ∼ Uniform(0, θ),  θ unknown

Two estimators

From one sample X1, …, Xn with n = 50, we will compare a rescaled average with the largest observation.

Estimator A
2X̄

The sample average estimates θ/2, so doubling it targets θ.

Estimator B
X(n) := max{Xi}

The largest observation uses the sample point nearest the unknown endpoint.
