Likelihood connects a model to observed data.

For parameters η, the log-likelihood is ℓ(η) = Σ log f(xᵢ | η). It is not a probability and can be negative. Here, maximize it while the data and model family stay fixed; raw maxima across different families do not adjust for model complexity.

  • Sampling varies: each new sample changes the histogram and best-fit parameters.
  • Parameters reshape: location, spread, rate, and shape alter the fitted density.
  • Support matters: assigning zero density to one observation makes the log-likelihood −∞.
